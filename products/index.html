GpsLite

GPS, without satellites…
iPhoneAndroidDead reckoning

GpsLite takes a single GPS reading to anchor your starting point, then tracks where your device goes using only its motion sensors: accelerometer and compass. It's a hands-on look at dead reckoning, the same idea ships and aircraft used long before satellites.

  • Automatically detects whether you're walking, driving or stopped, and adapts its tracking.
  • Live map shows the sensor-estimated path alongside a GPS reference path for comparison.
  • Replay past sessions with play, pause and scrub controls, and see how far the estimate drifted.
  • All sessions are stored locally on your device.
