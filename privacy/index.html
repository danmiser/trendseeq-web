Privacy Policy

Short version: we don't sell your data, show ads or track you. Most of our apps keep everything on your device, and where an app has to send something off it, we say exactly what and why.

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrendSeeq, LLC ("TrendSeeq", "we", "us") handles information in our mobile applications (the "Apps") and on www.trendseeq.com (the "Site").

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • Our Apps contain no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Recipe Engineer, GpsLite, CribbageScorer and JukeJams store your content only on your device. We never receive it.
  • ReplyMeme and VoxSeeq need online services to work. The sections below explain exactly what they send and where.
  • Device permissions (camera, location, motion, microphone, local network) are used only for the feature that asks for them.

Recipe Engineer

  • Your recipes are stored on your device only. They are included in your device's own backups (such as iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings.
  • Camera. If you photograph a recipe, the image is used to read its text. Photos are not sent to us.
  • Web import. When you import a recipe from a URL, the App downloads that page directly from the website you entered. That website will receive a standard web request from your device (including your IP address), governed by that site's own privacy policy. We do not receive the URL or the page.
  • Sharing. When you share a recipe, it goes only where you choose to send it, using your device's share sheet.

GpsLite

  • Location is used while a tracking session is active, to anchor the starting point and to record a GPS reference path for comparison.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope and compass) are used to estimate movement.
  • Session data is stored in a database on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete sessions at any time in the App; uninstalling removes them all.
  • Maps. The map is displayed using your platform's map service (Apple Maps on iOS, Google Maps on Android), which may receive map-tile requests for the area being viewed, subject to Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

ReplyMeme

  • The text you enter is sent to OpenAI to generate a reply, and the resulting caption is sent to Imgflip to create the meme image. Their handling of this data is governed by the OpenAI and Imgflip privacy policies. Under OpenAI's API terms, API data is not used to train its models by default.
  • Don't enter sensitive personal information (such as passwords, financial or health details) as message text.
  • We do not require an account and do not store your messages or generated memes on our own servers.
  • Sharing. Memes go only where you choose to send them, using your device's share sheet.

CribbageScorer

  • Camera. The camera is used to recognize playing cards. Recognition runs entirely on your device; images are not stored or sent anywhere.

JukeJams

  • Nearby devices. JukeJams connects directly to nearby iPhones over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth to share the playlist. Your device name and song requests are visible to the other people in the session.
  • Apple Music. Song search and playback use Apple's MusicKit, subject to Apple's privacy policy. Your Apple Music account details are never shared with us or other guests.
  • The playlist is cached only on your device. We do not operate servers for JukeJams.

VoxSeeq

  • Account. VoxSeeq requires an account so friends can find you. We store your sign-in identifier, display name and your connections.
  • Voice sample. With your permission, the microphone records a short voice sample. It is uploaded to VoxSeeq's server to create a voice profile used only to speak messages you send. We never use your voice for anything else, and never share or sell it.
  • Messages you send are processed on VoxSeeq's server to generate audio for the recipients you choose.
  • Deletion. You can delete your voice sample in the app at any time, or email us to delete your account and all associated data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment information. We may receive aggregated, non-identifying sales reports from Apple or Google.

This website

This Site is a static website. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operation. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

Children, security & your rights

Children. Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and encryption. Data sent to online services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access or delete personal information. For most of our Apps, there's nothing for us to provide or delete because your data stays on your device. For VoxSeeq, we will honor requests to access or delete your account and voice data. For any request, contact us below.

Consulting clients. Data handled as part of a consulting engagement is governed by that engagement's agreement, not this policy.

Changes & contact

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will be noted on this page.
